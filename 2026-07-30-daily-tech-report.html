Claude Mythos Preview Cracks HAWK-256 Post-Quantum Crypto in 60 Hours

Milestone

Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview autonomously discovered a fundamental flaw in HAWK, a NIST post-quantum cryptography candidate, in just 60 hours for ~$100K in compute. The attack exploits a previously unknown symmetry in HAWK's lattice structure, cutting effective key strength roughly in half (from ~2^64 to ~2^38 operations). The same model also invented a novel technique it named the "Möbius Bridge" that makes an existing attack on a weakened AES variant 200–800x faster. Humans provided project direction and verification, but the AI largely conducted the research itself.

Why it matters: This is a landmark demonstration of AI conducting original cryptographic research. While neither finding breaks deployed systems (HAWK was undeployed, and the AES result targets a research variant), it signals that AI can now find vulnerabilities human experts missed after two years of review. For agency owners: keep watching how AI-powered security auditing tools evolve.
via Anthropic Research

HAWK Withdrawn from NIST Post-Quantum Standardization

Important

Following Anthropic's published attack, the HAWK team confirmed the findings and voluntarily withdrew HAWK from NIST's additional post-quantum signature standardization process. The team stated that straightforward mitigations (doubling parameters or moving to higher-rank modules) would make HAWK uncompetitive with other candidates. NIST subsequently updated its third-round candidate page to mark HAWK as withdrawn.

Why it matters: An AI system directly influenced a NIST cryptographic standardization decision for the first time. This accelerates the post-quantum migration timeline and validates AI as a serious tool for cryptanalysis. If you handle sensitive client data or e-commerce, this reinforces why staying current on TLS and encryption standards matters.
via The Hacker News

Claude Experiences Global Outage — 529 Errors Hit Thousands

Breaking

On July 29, Claude went down worldwide starting at ~19:45 UTC, with over 2,000 outage reports on Downdetector. Requests failed with "529 Overloaded" errors across Claude.ai, Claude Code, and the API. Roughly half of reports involved Claude Code. Anthropic identified the issue at 20:33 UTC, and service was restored by ~21:26 UTC. The underlying cause was not disclosed.

Why it matters: If you rely on Claude Code for daily development workflows, yesterday's ~90-minute outage is a reminder to have fallback plans. Consider keeping a local LLM (like Ollama) configured as a backup for critical deadlines, and monitor status.anthropic.com during work hours.
