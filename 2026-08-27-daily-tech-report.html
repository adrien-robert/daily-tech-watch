Anthropic unifies Claude memory across Chat and Cowork

Update

Claude now shares a single memory pool between Chat and Cowork sessions. Anything discussed in conversation — project context, preferences, recurring priorities — is automatically available when Cowork performs cloud-based tasks, and vice versa. Memory now updates in real-time during conversations rather than waiting until the end. On by default for Free, Pro, and Max plans. Claude Code is not included.

Why it matters: For agency owners managing multiple client projects, this eliminates the "context re-explaining" tax. Discuss a client's brand guidelines in chat, then switch to Cowork to draft content or run tasks — Claude already knows the project. A meaningful quality-of-life improvement for daily workflows.

OpenAI's Jalapeño inference chip outperforms Nvidia Blackwell in first benchmarks

Milestone

OpenAI presented benchmark results for Jalapeño, its custom inference chip built with Broadcom, at Hot Chips on August 25. Self-reported numbers show 1.5–1.9x more throughput per watt versus Nvidia Blackwell, with 1.7–3.6x lower latency. SemiAnalysis independently verified the results. Caveat: Jalapeño uses newer HBM4 memory, making a direct comparison somewhat unfair. Small-scale deployment planned for late 2026.

Why it matters: Custom silicon competition between OpenAI, Google (TPU), and Amazon (Trainium) is driving down inference costs. Cheaper inference at scale benefits every downstream user — including agencies building AI-powered content pipelines and development workflows. Watch for pricing pressure across all LLM providers.
via CNBC

Amazon shuts down Mechanical Turk after 21 years

Milestone

Amazon announced it will shut down Mechanical Turk on September 30, 2026. The crowdsourced labor marketplace launched in 2005 and at its peak served over 500,000 workers performing data labeling, transcription, and survey tasks. Amazon stopped accepting new customers on July 30 and cited an internal review of programs. The platform had been in decline as AI-powered alternatives replaced many human-task workflows.

Why it matters: A symbolic end to the "human-in-the-loop" era. MTurk trained the first generation of ML models; now AI handles most of those tasks. For agencies or clients still using crowd-sourced data labeling, content moderation, or survey distribution through MTurk, the September 30 deadline is real — start migrating now.
