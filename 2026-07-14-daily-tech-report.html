Trend

EU AI Act: High-Risk Deadline Deferred 16 Months to December 2027

Important

The Council of the EU gave final approval on June 29 to the AI Act simplification package. The most significant change: Annex III high-risk AI system obligations are postponed from August 2, 2026, to December 2, 2027 — a 16-month deferral. Transparency obligations under Article 50, conformity assessments, and CE marking requirements are all pushed back. The legislation will enter into force shortly after publication in the Official Journal.

Why it matters: If you deploy AI features on client sites (chatbots, recommendation engines, AI-generated content), you now have until late 2027 to ensure compliance. Use this breathing room to build compliant-by-default templates and document your AI usage across client projects, rather than scrambling later.
via Latham & Watkins
