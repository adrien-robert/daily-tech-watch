ACRE Exoneration Dropping to 25% — July 1 Deadline for New Businesses

Deadline

Starting July 1, 2026, the ACRE (Aide aux Créateurs et Repreneurs d'Entreprise) exoneration for new micro-enterprises drops from 50% to 25% of social contributions. The aid is also being restricted to specific profiles: job seekers registered 6+ months, RSA/ASS beneficiaries, young people 18-25, and projects in rural revitalization zones (ZFRR). Applications must be submitted to URSSAF within 60 days of registration.

Why it matters: If you're advising clients on launching their own micro-entreprise or considering creating a secondary structure, any registration before July 1 locks in the current 50% rate. After that date, the benefit is halved and eligibility is much more restrictive.
