Google Launches Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD) Specification

New

Google announced an open specification for publishing, discovering, and verifying AI capabilities across the web. Why it matters: ARD solves the "how do agents find the right MCP server?" problem. For agency owners building AI-powered WordPress workflows, this means your custom MCP tools could eventually be discoverable by any compatible AI agent automatically, reducing integration friction.

Security

Google released Chrome 149.0.7827.155/.156 on June 17, patching critical and high-severity vulnerabilities.
