EU Cyber Resilience Act: Reporting Deadline in 7 Days (September 11)

Deadline

The EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) vulnerability and incident reporting obligations take effect on September 11, 2026. From that date, anyone selling digital products (including WordPress plugins and themes) to EU customers must report actively exploited vulnerabilities to ENISA within 24 hours, with a full notification within 72 hours. Non-compliance carries fines up to EUR 15 million or 2.5% of global turnover.

Why it matters: As a French agency, this directly affects you and your clients who distribute plugins or themes. Ensure you have a documented vulnerability reporting process, a security contact, and an SBOM (Software Bill of Materials) for any digital products you sell. Review client projects to confirm compliance.
