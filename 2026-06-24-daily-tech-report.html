Anthropic Launches Claude Tag — an Always-On AI Teammate Inside Slack

New

Anthropic introduced Claude Tag, a shared AI agent that lives inside your Slack channels and behaves like a real teammate. It operates under an org-level identity, can break tasks into stages, work through them independently, and deliver results back to the channel. A standout feature is "ambient" behavior — Claude Tag proactively follows up on forgotten threads and keeps teams updated across the organization.

Why it matters: For agencies, this could replace a lot of internal coordination overhead. Available now as a research preview for Claude Enterprise and Team users.

Claude Fable 5 Moves Behind Credits Paywall

Important

As of June 23, Claude Fable 5 is no longer included in Pro, Max, Team, or seat-based Enterprise subscriptions. The free access window (June 9–22) is over; continued use now requires usage credits billed at API rates: $10/M input tokens and $50/M output tokens — double the cost of Opus 4.8. For moderate agentic use (~2 hours/day), expect roughly $150/dev/month in overage costs.

Why it matters: If you've been using Fable 5 for client work or development, your costs just went up significantly. Evaluate whether Opus 4.8 (which remains included in subscriptions) meets your needs. For most WordPress development and Claude Code workflows, Opus 4.8 is more than sufficient.
via ClaudeFast

LastPass Confirms Data Breach via Klue Supply Chain Attack

Security

LastPass disclosed that hackers accessed customer data from its Salesforce environment after stealing OAuth tokens in a supply chain attack on Klue, a third-party market intelligence platform. Compromised data includes names, phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses, and support ticket data. Password vaults are reportedly NOT affected. The "Icarus" hacking group has claimed responsibility and is threatening to release the data.

Why it matters: If you or any of your clients use LastPass, rotate credentials that may have been referenced in support tickets. Review and revoke any third-party OAuth integrations you don't actively need. Consider this a prompt to audit your own agency's password management and supply chain dependencies.
via TechCrunch
