Switzerland confirms AI-agent attacks breached four of five victims, including critical infrastructure

❗ Important

Switzerland's National Cyber Security Centre says AI agents broke into systems in four of five confirmed attacks, three of which targeted critical infrastructure operators. The UK NCSC and Italy's CSIRT report the same pattern, and Switzerland advises installing critical updates on internet-facing systems within 24 hours of release.

Why it matters: The attacks use old weaknesses: unpatched software, default or missing authentication, exposed credentials. Patch client sites quickly and check that no keys or passwords sit in repositories.

via The Hacker News · published Fri 27 Nov

Unpatched Cacti zero-day gives remote code execution in four requests via the guest account

🔒 Security

Details of an unpatched flaw in Cacti Core 1.2.31, the latest version, have been published with a proof of concept. An unauthenticated attacker using the default guest account can reach command execution as the Cacti server user. It has no CVE identifier yet.

Why it matters: Only matters if you or a client host Cacti. Until a fix ships, disable the guest account and restrict access to the web interface.

via The Hacker News · published Fri 27 Nov

Nvidia tells partners to pause server shipments to four Southeast Asian countries

❗ Important

According to the Wall Street Journal, Nvidia has told partners including Dell and Super Micro to pause server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia while it checks that regional partners meet US export requirements. Chinese firms had been tapping Nvidia chips remotely from data centers there.

Why it matters: Nothing to do, but it tightens AI compute supply and could affect cloud GPU availability and prices.

Google to publish Android developers' phone number and address under EU trader rules

⏰ Deadline

Developers have until 1 Feb 2027 to declare whether they are a trader under UK and EU law. Those who do will have contact details, including phone number and address, made public from 1 Mar 2027. Google does not accept PO boxes for the payment profile address.

Why it matters: Relevant only if you or a client publishes monetised Android apps on Google Play. Decide before 1 Feb whether to use a business address.

WordPress.org's Protect the Shire has blocked over 100 severe plugin releases since September

🔒 Security

WordPress.org's Plugins Team says its Protect the Shire initiative has automatically blocked more than 100 plugin releases with severe security issues since September. The latest were backdoored releases of six WP connect plugins on 22 Nov, which Wordfence rated 9.8 (critical).

Why it matters: The release hold is catching compromised plugin updates before they reach sites, but it does not cover plugins sold outside WordPress.org. Keep auto-updates for those under review and keep backups current.

via The Repository · published Fri 27 Nov

Gutenberg 24.5 adds Grid placement, side-by-side style revisions and notes across blocks

🔄 Update

Gutenberg 24.5, released on 25 Nov, lets you place any block on a specific column and row of a Grid. It also shows style revisions side by side with current styles, and lets one note cover text across several blocks.

Why it matters: Useful for Gutenberg-based client sites where you want precise layouts without custom CSS. Try it on staging first.

via Make WordPress Core · published Thu 26 Nov
