France E-Invoicing Mandate Now Active — September 1, 2026

Deadline

As of September 1, 2026, all French companies subject to VAT must be able to receive electronic invoices. Large and medium-sized enterprises must also emit e-invoices now; small and micro-enterprises (including EURLs) must emit by September 1, 2027. Invoices must use Factur-X, UBL, or CII formats via approved platforms (PDP). All businesses must also report transaction data (e-reporting) starting now.

Why it matters: Your EURL must now be able to receive e-invoices from suppliers and larger clients. You have until September 2027 to emit, but setting up your approved platform (PDP) now avoids a last-minute scramble. Check that your accounting software supports Factur-X.
via URSSAF
