MCP Protocol Goes Fully Stateless — Biggest Spec Update Since Launch

Important

The 2026-07-28 MCP specification, now stewarded by the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation, converts MCP from a bidirectional stateful protocol to request/response stateless. The initialize handshake and Mcp-Session-Id header are removed. Every request now carries its own protocol version and client identity.

Why it matters: Stateless MCP means simpler deployment — any server instance can handle any request behind a standard load balancer. If you maintain custom MCP servers (e.g., for WordPress), you'll need to update them to the new spec. The update also closes OAuth authorization vulnerabilities.
via VentureBeat

EU AI Act Article 50: Transparency Obligations Now Enforceable

Important

As of August 2, 2026, the EU AI Act's transparency obligations under Article 50 are enforceable. AI systems interacting with users (chatbots, AI agents) must disclose they're AI. AI-generated content (text, image, audio, video) must be machine-readable as AI-generated. Non-compliance penalties: up to 15M EUR or 3% of global turnover. Generative AI providers already on the market get until December 2 to comply with the output-marking requirement.

Why it matters: If you deploy AI chatbots or generate AI content for client sites in the EU, you need to disclose AI involvement. Review any AI-powered features on client sites — chatbots, content generators, recommendation engines — and ensure they display appropriate disclosures. As an EURL in France, this applies directly to you.
