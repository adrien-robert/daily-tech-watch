EU Cyber Resilience Act: Plugin Devs Must Have Vulnerability Disclosure by September 2026

Deadline

The EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) requires all plugin and theme developers distributing software to EU users to have vulnerability disclosure programs (VDP) in place by September 2026. Developers must report actively exploited vulnerabilities to ENISA and national CSIRTs, maintain component lists, and track dependencies with upstream notification duties.

Why it matters: If you develop or distribute custom plugins/themes to EU clients, you need a VDP now. This also affects which third-party plugins you recommend — non-compliant plugins could become a liability for your agency and your clients.
